# Spineboy

Esoteric Software's own example skeleton, from
`examples/spineboy/export` of https://github.com/EsotericSoftware/spine-runtimes
(branch 4.3). Not ours: the Spine Runtimes examples are licensed for use with
Spine and are **not** free for commercial use — see Esoteric Software's terms.

It is here so `nx.py new <name> -t spine/skeleton` produces something that runs.
If you are shipping, replace it with your own export and delete this directory.
